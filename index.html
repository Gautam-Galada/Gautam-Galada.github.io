I study what language models infer about the people they talk to, and whether the rules we write for AI show up in how models actually compute.

I’m a PhD student at the University of North Texas, working with the Lawful Computing Lab. My research sits between mechanistic interpretability and lawful AI: I look inside language models to see how they represent their users, and test whether legal constraints such as privacy law reach that internal computation, or only the surface of what a model says.

Before UNT, I earned my Master’s at the University at Buffalo and my undergraduate degree at VIT-AP. Away from the desk I read physics and mathematics for fun, and spend weekends on long trails with a camera.
